# Farming With Souls

## The Real Positioning

Unborn is still a **farming system**.

We generate presence, activity, and attention at scale — the same job existing device farms and engagement farms try to do.

The difference is what sits behind every single instance:

> **Behind every farmed body there is a Persona (a soul).**

That single fact is the entire moat.

---

## How Normal Farms Get 1 Lakh Views

Typical approach today:

1. Spin up large numbers of accounts/devices
2. Run generic or lightly varied scripts
3. Spray likes, views, comments, follows
4. Accept high burn rate (bans, shadowbans, resets)
5. Keep replacing dead accounts with new ones
6. Race to the bottom on price per action

Result:
- High short-term volume possible
- Low authenticity
- High detection risk
- Constant churn
- No compounding value (every account is disposable)

---

## How Unborn Gets Meaningful Volume

Same goal (attention at scale), different engine:

1. Every body is bound to a coherent Persona
2. Activity is generated from personality, taste, circadian rhythm, memory, and engagement style
3. Vitality system applies real consequences — bad patterns get pressure, good patterns survive
4. Personas accumulate history and become more valuable over time instead of being thrown away
5. Population-level controls keep a group of personas from looking coordinated
6. Because survival is higher and behavior is more believable, the same number of actions produces more sustainable, higher-quality attention

We still farm.  
We just stop farming with empty shells.

---

## The Moat in One Sentence

**Other farms rent you activity.  
Unborn runs a living population of people who generate activity.**

Later, those same people (the Personas) can be reused for:
- Conversation
- Tool use via MCP
- Research / synthetic users
- Community presence
- Any future surface

The farm is the first body. The Persona is the lasting asset.

---

## Commercial Implication

We compete in the farming category, but we refuse the pure disposable-volume game.

What we sell:
- Larger living populations (tens → hundreds → more)
- Higher survival
- Higher perceived authenticity
- Lower long-term waste (fewer dead accounts)
- The option to reuse the same Personas beyond the original farm surface

What we still must deliver:
- Real scale of attention (1 lakh views class outcomes must be possible)
- Competitive density and cost structure over time
- Operational simplicity for the customer (self-hosted farm that actually runs)

---

## Engineering Consequence

The system has two jobs that must both be true at once:

1. **Farm at useful scale** (many concurrent bodies, real output)
2. **Never let the souls become empty** (coherence, memory, distinctiveness, Vitality)

If we only do #1, we are just another farm.  
If we only do #2, we have beautiful personas that don’t move the needle.

Both are required.

---

## Updated North Star Metric

Not “how perfect is one persona”.  
Not “how many empty accounts can we run”.

**How much sustainable, believable attention can a living population of N personas generate — and how long do they keep doing it without collapsing.**

That is the number that matters for revenue.
