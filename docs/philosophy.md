# Unborn Philosophy

## Purpose

We are not building a “responsible social media tool”.  
We are building an **unprecedented, transcendent capability**: high-fidelity, persistent, coherent synthetic humans.

The purpose of the core engine is power and fidelity.  
Nothing else.

---

## Modular Responsibility (Non-Negotiable)

Every major concern is a **separate module** with clean boundaries.

- The Persona Engine does one thing: create and sustain the most coherent, long-lived, believable digital people possible.
- The Behavior Engine does one thing: turn persona into action with maximum fidelity.
- The Identity & Sensor systems do one thing: make the body match the soul perfectly.
- The Detection Radar does one thing: see trouble early and adapt.
- Guardrails / Ethics / Policy enforcement is a **separate module** that sits above or beside the core.

**Core modules must be allowed to run at full power.**  
No crippling the engine from the inside “just in case”.

Responsibility is segregated so that:
1. Each system can be pushed to the extreme of its purpose.
2. Constraints (ethical, legal, customer policy) can be added, removed, or evolved without rewriting the soul of the product.
3. Different customers can apply different constraint layers later without forcing the lowest common denominator on everyone.

---

## Guardrails Philosophy

We will never build impersonation of real private individuals into the core product.  
That capability, if it ever exists, belongs in a clearly separated, heavily controlled module (or not at all).

For now:
- Core engine = maximum fidelity and power
- Guardrails = separate layer that will come later
- The existence of future guardrails must not weaken the design of the core

This is how you build something transcendent instead of something timid.

---

## Design Consequence

When we face a trade-off inside a core module:

- Prefer the more powerful, more coherent, more long-term solution.
- Do not pre-emptively nerf capability because of hypothetical misuse.
- Keep interfaces clean so a policy layer can later constrain outputs or actions without touching the internal excellence of the engine.

The goal is not the most restricted product.  
The goal is the most capable product that can later accept whatever constraints the world (or the customer) requires.

---

## One-Sentence North Star

**Build the most powerful and coherent synthetic human engine possible. Keep responsibility modular so power and constraint never destroy each other.**
