# Unborn – Product Strategy & Moat Decisions
(Locked for next 12 months)

## Core Decision: What We Actually Sell

We sell **believable, long-lived digital people of a specific niche**.

Not:
- Views
- Engagements
- Device farms
- “Anti-detect” tools

Customers pay because real humans in their community start treating these personas as genuine members. That social proof is extremely high-value.

---

## Persona Requirements (Non-Negotiable)

Every persona **must** have:

1. **History / Memory**
   - Short-term: recent interactions, what they just saw/liked/commented
   - Long-term: evolving preferences, opinions, “relationships” with content/accounts
   - This is what prevents them from feeling brand new or random

2. **Presence**
   - Consistent circadian rhythm and activity windows
   - Feels like they “have always been around” rather than appearing in bursts
   - Physical context continuity (how they hold the phone, when they are walking vs sitting, etc.)

3. **Distinct Personality within Niche**
   - Even in a population of “Berlin design-loving creatives”, each one feels like a different person
   - Different engagement philosophies (lurker / thoughtful commenter / selective sharer)

4. **Coherent Multi-Modal Signals**
   - Language style + visual attention + dwell times + sensor behavior + timing all match the same human

Without the above, it is just another bot farm. With them, it becomes something people actually want to pay for.

---

## The Real Moat (What Makes Money in 12 Months)

Ranked by importance for revenue in the next year:

### 1. Superior Persona Coherence & Longevity (Primary Moat)
- Personas that survive weeks/months and feel real to actual humans
- Hard to copy because it requires deep integration of identity + behavior + memory + sensors
- Customers will pay a premium for “these people don’t die and don’t feel fake”

### 2. Natural Language Persona Studio
- “Give me 12 quiet, thoughtful 40+ readers who engage with long-form writing”
- Extremely high willingness-to-pay from non-technical buyers (agencies, brand managers, community leads)
- Low competition currently

### 3. Population-Level Realism
- Ability to deploy a small coherent community from one niche that doesn’t look coordinated
- Very valuable for seeding communities or social proof

### 4. Detection Radar + Adaptive Survival
- Early warning + automatic graceful adaptation
- Turns into a reliability feature customers pay for

### Secondary (nice later)
- Cross-platform consistency
- Customer-specific fine-tuning
- Full synthetic data flywheel

---

## Business Model That Can Actually Work in 12 Months

**Primary:** Self-hosted engine + subscription for the Persona Intelligence layer

Pricing axes that make sense:
- Number of active long-lived personas
- Niche complexity / custom DNA
- Duration of persona life (longevity is a feature)
- Access to Natural Language Studio + Detection Radar

**High-willingness-to-pay segments (focus here first):**
1. Marketing / growth agencies running community seeding for clients
2. Brands building or defending niche communities
3. Creator tools / platforms that want authentic-looking engagement
4. Private / paid communities that want to feel alive

Avoid pure “growth hackers who just want cheap likes”. They will churn and create detection risk.

---

## Stress Tests (Anti-Vaporware Rules)

We will kill or heavily deprioritize anything that fails these:

1. **Will a real customer pay for this in the next 6–9 months?**
2. **Does this increase the chance that personas feel real to actual humans?**
3. **Does this help personas survive longer?**
4. **Can this be delivered without requiring a research lab?**

### Explicitly rejected for next 12 months
- Over-perfect simulation that delays shipping
- Features that only matter if you already have 10k instances
- Anything that makes the product look like a classic bot farm
- Building for every possible platform before we have one that works extremely well

---

## Phase Priority for Revenue

**Now → 3 months:** Phase 1 MVP that is actually installable and usable
**3–7 months:** Strong Persona system + basic Natural Language control + longevity
**7–12 months:** Population realism + Detection Radar maturity + first serious paying customers

The goal is not “most advanced research project”.  
The goal is **the first product in this category that people willingly pay for because the people feel real**.
